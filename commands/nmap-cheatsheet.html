Nmap Cheatsheet

Battle-tested nmap one-liners for discovery, version detection and scripts.

Quick top ports
$ nmap -sV --top-ports 100 -T4 <target>
Full TCP + scripts
$ nmap -sC -sV -p- -T4 <target> -oA full
UDP common
$ nmap -sU --top-ports 50 <target>
OS detection
$ nmap -O -sV <target>
